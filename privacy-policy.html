Learn how Pass4Job collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

When you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Phone Number
  • Company Name (if applicable)

Technical Information

  • Browser Type

  • Pages Visited

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide IT training services and support

  • Respond to inquiries and customer service requests

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

Requests may be submitted through our contact information.

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
